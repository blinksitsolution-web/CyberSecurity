# Personal next-step plan

## Goal

Leave the bootcamp with a plan that matches what you can actually do next. Friday's submission includes this plan beside the mock and the presentation. Week 1's learning plan is the document you are closing.

## What was built

`next-steps.md` asks for 30 days, 90 days, and what you will not pretend. It does not assign you a job title or a certification result.

## How to reproduce

1. Re-read your Week 1 checkpoint table and fill any weeks you skipped, honestly.
2. Choose one portfolio gap that matters for SOC work: a real Wazuh screenshot, a restore test, or a detection you generated on your VM.
3. Write the next step so a stranger could tell whether you did it.
4. Add public links only if they are yours.
