# Capstone outline

## Goal

Plan the mini-project presentation: a home SOC lab that can explain two detections, the evidence behind them, and the limits of what you built. Friday is presentation day. The timetable also includes peer feedback.

## What was built

`outline.md` is a talk structure aimed at blue-team work. Suggested timings fit a short presentation. Every result slot says "your evidence" so you cannot submit the outline as if the lab were finished.

## How to reproduce

1. Choose a title and write your name.
2. For each slide, point at a file in this repository or at a screenshot you took.
3. Rehearse once and note the question you could not answer. That question goes on the limits slide, not into a made-up metric.
4. Ask a teammate for one piece of feedback and record it.
