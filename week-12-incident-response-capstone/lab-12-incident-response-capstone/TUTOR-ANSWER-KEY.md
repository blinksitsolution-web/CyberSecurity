# Tutor answer key — Lab 12

Not part of the trainee PDF.

The shipped `timeline.csv` fails `timeline_check.py` until he adds ordered UTC rows with a source each. A valid timeline moves forward and cites a log, a command, or a snapshot name.

The simulation actions are a wrong SSH password he chooses and a `lab12user` he adds to `sudo`, then locks or deletes. Success is proof the account is gone or the VM was reverted to `before-lab-12`, plus a report whose banner is `my-lab` or `PRACTICE DATA`. The Lab 11 fixture is practice data, not a breach.

There is no hidden attacker write-up. Grade the limits slide by whether it admits something the lab did not see.
