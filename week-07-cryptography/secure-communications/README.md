# Secure communications

## Goal

Explain TLS and IPsec, and give a small demo of what you can show without attacking anything. Friday's submission is this explanation.

## What was built

`tls-ipsec-explainer.md` is the explanation and a demo script that reuses the certificate inspector. There is no packet capture of a third party and no instruction for intercepting traffic.

## How to reproduce

1. Read the explainer and rewrite the final section in your own words. The draft is there so you have a structure, not so you can submit it untouched.
2. Generate the lab certificate from the certificate project and point at the fields a person should check before trusting a dashboard.
3. If you open your own Wazuh dashboard, record the name in the browser warning or the certificate, in your own screenshot.
