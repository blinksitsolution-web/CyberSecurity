# 5 by 5 risk matrix

Score = likelihood × impact. Copy an id into one cell after you have both numbers. A risk sits in one cell only.

Likelihood increases to the right. Impact increases upward. The top-right corner is the dangerous corner.

## Bands

| Score | Band | What you do in this lab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Low | Record it. A named person may accept it. |
| 6–11 | Medium | Give it an owner and a treatment. |
| 12–19 | High | Flag it. Do not leave the treatment blank. |
| 20–25 | Critical | Flag it. Say what happens this week, not "someday". |

The script flags **12 and above**. That is high and critical.

## Colour, if you want it

You do not have to colour the grid. If you do: low green, medium yellow, high orange, critical red. Colour does not change the score.

## Check against the script

The cell you filled by hand should match the cell the script prints. If they disagree, the CSV and this grid have different numbers. Fix the one that does not match your written reason.
