# Risk register

## Goal

Practise a risk statement: an asset, a threat, a vulnerability, and a control. You used this in the Wednesday practical when you mapped assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and controls.

## What was built

`risk-register.csv` is a working register. Six rows are a teaching example for a fictional study hub. They are marked `example=yes` and the scores are illustrative, not a measurement of a real place. Blank rows are where your own lab assets go.

Words used in the file:

- **Asset** — something you want to protect.
- **Threat** — something that can cause harm.
- **Vulnerability** — a weakness the threat can use.
- **Control** — something that reduces the chance or the harm.
- **CIA** — confidentiality, integrity, availability. Record which of the three the risk hits first.

## How to reproduce

1. Open `risk-register.csv` in a spreadsheet or a text editor.
2. Read the example rows, then add your own rows with `example=no`.
3. Use a 1–5 score only when you can say why. Leave the score blank if you are guessing.
4. In Week 4 you will extend this into a scored sprint. Do not delete these rows; copy the ones you still believe.
