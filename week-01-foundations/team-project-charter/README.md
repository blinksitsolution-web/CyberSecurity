# Team project charter

## Goal

Agree how the project team will work before the technical work piles up. The charter covers roles, decisions, missed deadlines, and the rule that labs stay on systems you are allowed to test.

## What was built

`charter.md` is the agreement template. Names, roles, and signatures are blank on purpose.

## How to reproduce

1. Fill `charter.md` with your real team.
2. Read the lab-safety section out loud once. Change wording if the team needs a clearer rule, without weakening the permission rule.
3. Each person adds their name and the date they agreed.
