# Ethics case studies and Ghana law notes

## Goal

Discuss ethical and legal cases in a team: authorised practice, disclosure, and the use of AI. The Friday session pairs professional ethics with Ghana's legal boundaries. This folder is study material, not legal advice.

## What was built

- `ghana-law-notes.md` — short, sourced notes on Act 1038, Act 843, Act 772, and the Bank of Ghana Cyber and Information Security Directive.
- `case-studies.md` — five discussion cases with questions and no model verdict. Your group's answer is the deliverable.

## How to reproduce

1. Read the law notes and open the official texts linked there when you need the exact section.
2. For each case, write what your team would do, what you would refuse, and which law or principle you are leaning on.
3. Mark anything you are unsure about as a question for the trainer.
