# Ghana law notes for Week 1

These are study notes for class. They are not legal advice, and they are not a substitute for the official text. Read the current document before you quote a section number in an assignment.

## Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038)

Act 1038 establishes the Cyber Security Authority. Its objects include regulating cybersecurity activities, preventing and responding to cybersecurity incidents, regulating owners of critical information infrastructure and cybersecurity service providers, and promoting a safer digital environment. The Authority also promotes the protection of children online and can license specified cybersecurity services.

Official text: [Cybersecurity Act, 2020 on GhaLII](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2020/1038/eng@2020-12-29). The Cyber Security Authority publishes material at [csa.gov.gh](https://www.csa.gov.gh/).

Why it matters in a SOC: incident reporting, critical systems, and who is allowed to offer cybersecurity services are not only company rules.

## Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)

Act 843 establishes the Data Protection Commission. It protects privacy by regulating how personal information is processed. Section 17 names eight principles:

1. Accountability
2. Lawfulness of processing
3. Specification of purpose
4. Compatibility of further processing with the purpose of collection
5. Quality of information
6. Openness
7. Data security safeguards
8. Data subject participation

Official text: [Data Protection Act, 2012 on GhaLII](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2012/843/eng@2012-05-18).

Why it matters in a SOC: logs, screenshots, and ticket exports often contain personal data. Collect what you need, store it safely, and do not paste it into a public chatbot or a public repository.

## Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772)

Act 772 regulates electronic communications and related transactions. It was amended by the Electronic Transactions (Amendment) Act, 2012 (Act 838) and by the Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038). Its cyber-offence provisions include unauthorised access to or interception of an electronic record.

Official text: [Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 on GhaLII](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2008/772/eng@2020-12-29).

Why it matters in a lab: "I was only looking" is not a scope. Access without authority is an offence, not a homework shortcut.

## Bank of Ghana Cyber and Information Security Directive

The October 2018 directive applies to institutions licensed or registered by the Bank of Ghana, including banks and other regulated entities covered by the directive. Its objectives include a safer environment for financial services, cyber and information security assessment, resilience of financial systems, and ongoing risk assessment.

The Bank of Ghana has also published a revised Cyber and Information Security Directive (2026). Use the current PDF on [bog.gov.gh](https://www.bog.gov.gh/) when you cite it. Do not rely on a classmate's summary for article numbers.

Why it matters if you want financial-sector blue-team work: governance, incident handling, and evidence are supervisory expectations, not optional extras.
