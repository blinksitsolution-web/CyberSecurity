# Personal learning plan

## Goal

Write a plan you can keep for twelve weeks. The bootcamp runs from 5 October to 25 December 2026. Your direction in this portfolio is blue team work: detection and log analysis, on a laptop with 32 GB of RAM.

## What was built

`learning-plan.md` is a blank plan with the prompts your trainer asked for. Nothing in it is filled in with a personal story, because that part is yours.

## How to reproduce

1. Copy `learning-plan.md` if you want a clean file, or edit it in place.
2. Answer every prompt. Short honest answers are better than a polished paragraph you do not mean.
3. At the end of each week, add one line to the checkpoint table.
