# Least-privilege proposal

## Goal

Propose one improvement that removes standing access you do not need. Friday's lab asks for this proposal after you have mapped the journeys and the matrix.

## What was built

`proposal.md` suggests a starting point for the home lab: separate the daily Windows user from the local administrator, and require a second person to approve a new sudo or Administrators member. The suggestion is a proposal, not a claim you have already changed the VM.

## How to reproduce

1. Pick the suggestion, or replace it with a better one from your own matrix.
2. Fill the before and after columns.
3. Name what you will log so Week 11's "new admin" detection has a reason to exist.
4. Do the change on the VM only when you are ready, then record the before and after evidence. Until then the status stays `proposed`.
