# Scoring guide

Use integers from 1 to 5. Score = likelihood × impact. A 5×5 grid tops out at 25.

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unlikely this term | A nuisance, quickly undone |
| 2 | Could happen | A class afternoon is disrupted |
| 3 | Plausible this month | Data or a lab machine is meaningfully harmed |
| 4 | Expected if nothing changes | Personal data leaves your control, or the lab is down for days |
| 5 | Already happening, or almost certain | Serious harm you would escalate the same day |

## Treatments

| Treatment | Meaning | Lab example, not a finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mitigate | Add or fix a control | Enrol the Windows VM with the Wazuh agent |
| Avoid | Stop the activity | Do not bridge the lab onto the home LAN |
| Transfer | Share the impact with someone else, often by contract or insurance | A school pays a provider to host mail. You still own how you use it |
| Accept | The remaining risk is small enough, and someone records that choice | A short lab window with a snapshot and no personal data |

Accept is a decision with a name attached. An empty cell is not acceptance.

## Worked sentence

"Likelihood 3 and impact 4 because an unencrypted laptop can leave the classroom, and the file on it has contact details. Treatment: mitigate, by keeping lab notes off that laptop until disk encryption is on. Owner: lab lead."

That sentence is a format example. Use your own asset and your own reasons.
